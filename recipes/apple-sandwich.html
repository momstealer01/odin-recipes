Apple Sandwich

Description

An apple sandwich is a versatile and nutritious snack or meal that typically features slices of apples paired with spreadable fillings and other ingredients layered between bread or between apple slices themselves. The combination offers a balance of sweet, savory, and crunchy textures

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ tablespoons creamy peanut butter
  • 2 slices apple
  • 2 tablespoons granola, or more to taste
