Brownies

Description

Brownies are a classic chocolate dessert known for their rich, fudgy, or cakey texture and deep cocoa flavor. They are typically made with simple pantry ingredients and are easy to prepare at home.

Ingredients

  • baking spray
  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup walnut halves
