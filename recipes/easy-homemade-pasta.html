Easy Homemade Pasta

Description

Homemade pasta is a simple dough made primarily from flour and eggs, sometimes with a bit of olive oil and salt for flavor and texture. It requires minimal ingredients and straightforward preparation, making fresh pasta accessible without specialized equipment.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 3 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons water, or as needed
